# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members of the QuranMediaLib community are committed to fostering an open and welcoming environment. With that in mind, we pledge to make positive contributions towards making our community a safe place for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- The use of stereotyped gender or racial slurs.
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other forms of unwelcome sexual attention or advances.

## Enforcement
Community members who engage in unacceptable behavior may be banned from the project.
